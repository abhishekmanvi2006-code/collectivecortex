# 🌐 Collective Cortex
## AI-Powered Government Scheme Assistance & Transparent Fund Monitoring Platform

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Collective Cortex is an AI-powered civic-tech platform designed to:

- Simplify access to government welfare schemes
- Guide citizens through application processes
- Improve transparency in government fund utilization
- Detect suspicious financial activities using AI
- Enable geo-tagged progress verification for public projects

This project combines:
- 🏠 Government Scheme Assistance
- 🎓 Education Loan Guidance
- 📄 Land Ownership Support
- 💰 Public Fund Transparency
- 🤖 AI Chatbot Support
- 🚨 AI Fraud Detection
- 📍 Geo-Tagged Work Monitoring

---

# 🚀 Features

---

## 1️⃣ Government Scheme Assistance Portal

Users can:

✅ Search government schemes  
✅ Check eligibility conditions  
✅ View required documents  
✅ Follow step-by-step guidance  
✅ Watch multilingual tutorial videos  
✅ Access official application websites  
✅ Ask doubts through AI chatbot support  

---

## 📋 Included Schemes

### 🏠 PMAY – Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana
Housing assistance scheme for eligible citizens.

### 📄 Land Ownership Certification
Guidance for obtaining land ownership certification.

### 🎓 Vidya Lakshmi Education Loan Scheme
Educational loan support and guidance.

### 🌞 PM-KUSUM Yojana
Solar agriculture and renewable energy support scheme.
